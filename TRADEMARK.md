# Eiraman Trademark Policy

*Draft, version 0.1.*

The Eiraman Guide, Role Profiles and tools are open. You can copy, change and share them under their licences (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). The Eiraman **name** and **logo** are different. They tell people that something comes from, or is approved by, the Eiraman project. This policy explains how you may use them so that the name keeps meaning something for everyone.

This policy is modelled on the [Linux Foundation Trademark Usage Guidelines](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/legal/trademark-usage), the [Rust Foundation Trademark Policy](https://rustfoundation.org/policies/logo-policy-and-media-guide/) (itself derived in part from the Python Software Foundation policy) and the [OpenInfra Foundation Trademark Policy](https://openinfra.org/legal/trademark-policy/).

## 1. The marks

- The word **Eiraman** (the "Name").
- The Eiraman logo and visual identity in `brand/` of the [`site`](https://github.com/eiraman/site/tree/main/brand) repository (the "Logo"), with its provenance in `brand/PROVENANCE.md`.

Together, the "Marks". Paolo Ungari, the project Steward (the "Steward"), claims rights in the Marks through their use for the project. The Marks are **not registered**, and the scope of protection of an unregistered mark depends on the applicable law. Do not use the ® symbol with them. Use ™ only if the Steward asks for it. This policy is separate from the copyright licences: neither CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY 4.0 nor the Apache License 2.0 gives any right to use the Marks.

## 2. General rules

- Be truthful. Do not suggest that the project made, endorses, certifies or is affiliated with you, your product or your service when it does not.
- Use the Name as an adjective with a noun ("the Eiraman Guide", "Eiraman Role Profiles"). Do not change its spelling, abbreviate it or combine it with other words into a new name.
- Make your own name more prominent than the Name.
- Do not use the Marks in a way that is misleading, offensive or harmful to the project's reputation.

## 3. Uses that need no permission

1. **Reference.** Saying true things about Eiraman in articles, books, talks, courses, documentation, research and social media, for example "our team applies the Eiraman Guide" or "an introduction to Eiraman".
2. **Compatibility.** Accurate statements such as "<product> is compatible with Eiraman Role Profiles" or "<tool> for Eiraman", as long as they do not suggest the product is official. The statement must refer to a specific version of the Guide or of the Role Profile format, for example "compatible with Eiraman Guide v1.0".
3. **Derived works under the open licences.** Saying that your work is "based on the Eiraman Guide" or "adapted from an Eiraman Role Profile", as part of the attribution the licences require. A modified version must not be presented as the Eiraman Guide itself or as an official Role Profile.
4. **Community events and groups.** Free, non-commercial meetups, study groups, workshops and online communities that discuss Eiraman, named for example "Milan Eiraman meetup" or "AI governance day featuring Eiraman". Say on the event page that it is a community event, not organised by the project.
5. **Repositories and packages.** Names such as `eiraman-profile-<topic>` or `<tool>-eiraman` for projects that work with Eiraman, provided their description does not claim official status.
6. **Linking.** Using the unmodified Logo to link to https://eiraman.tech or to the project repositories.
7. **Personal merchandise.** Stickers, shirts and similar items made for yourself and friends, not for sale.

## 4. Uses that need written permission

Ask the Steward before you:

1. **Certify or qualify.** Offer any certification, qualification, accreditation, badge or exam that uses the Name, for example "Eiraman Certified Manager" or "Eiraman Practitioner". The project does not run a certification programme today. Statements that a Role Profile is "qualified" are reserved to the process described in the Guide.
2. **Name a product, service or company.** Use the Name or Logo in, or as part of, the name of a product, service, company, domain name, social media account or app.
3. **Brand training or consulting.** Use the Name in the title of a paid course, training brand, consulting offer or published book series, beyond descriptive reference such as "a course on the Eiraman Guide".
4. **Hold commercial or sponsored events.** Run a commercial or sponsored event with the Name in its title, or use names reserved for official events, such as "EiramanCon".
5. **Use a modified Logo.** Change the Logo, combine it with another logo or use it in your own branding.
6. **Sell merchandise.** Sell or give away commercially any item with the Marks.
7. **Register.** File a trademark, business name or domain name that contains or resembles the Marks.

Requests go to the address in `community/CONTACT.md` *(to be created)*. Permission is given in writing, may carry conditions and can be withdrawn if the conditions are not met.

## 5. Uses that are never allowed

- Claiming that the project endorses, certifies or partners with you when it does not.
- Using a confusingly similar name or logo.
- Using the Marks for material that conflicts with the Guide's principles, such as presenting the removal of human approval as "Eiraman".

## 6. Transfer to a foundation

The Steward intends to transfer the Marks, and responsibility for this policy, to a neutral foundation or similar body once the thresholds in `GOVERNANCE.md` are met. When that happens:

- the foundation takes over the Steward's rights in the Marks and replaces the Steward in this policy;
- written permissions already granted remain valid under their conditions, unless the foundation tells the holder otherwise with reasonable notice;
- the foundation may change this policy through its own governance.

## 7. Changes and questions

The Steward may update this policy. Changes are published in the `community` repository with their date. If you are unsure whether your use is allowed, ask before you use the Marks.
