# Case studies

Anonymised case studies from adopters of Eiraman. They calibrate the open thresholds of the
applicability levels and count toward the stage thresholds in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md).
Owner: Adoption & Evidence working group.

## How to submit

1. Open an "Adopter case" issue in this repository.
2. Copy [CASE-STUDY-TEMPLATE.md](../CASE-STUDY-TEMPLATE.md) to `cases/<year>-<short-label>.md`.
   Use an anonymised project label, never a client or employer name.
3. Remove personal data, credentials, local paths and internal identifiers before you commit.
4. Open a pull request. Two maintainers of Adoption & Evidence review it, one of them not the author;
   evidence derived from a real project also needs a Privacy reviewer.

No case study has been accepted yet.
