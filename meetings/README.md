# Meeting notes

Public notes of working group and Steering Committee meetings, one file per meeting:
`meetings/<group>/<yyyy-mm-dd>.md`.

Each note records: attendees (handles), agenda, decisions with their reasons and the door type
(one-way or two-way), actions with an owner, and open points. Silence is not consent: a decision that
needs someone who was absent stays open until that person answers (GOVERNANCE §1).

Do not record personal data beyond handles, and nothing said under the Code of Conduct process.
